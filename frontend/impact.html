Pilot & Impact

From four counties to a national model.

Rebuild Trust Kenya launches as a pilot in four counties, tests and refines its model with real communities, then scales deliberately — through training-of-trainers and local partnerships, not just funding.

Pilot Implementation

Where the work starts.

The pilot runs across four counties, each with its own coordination structure but a shared, standardized facilitation model.
