Roadmap

Where we're headed.

Phase 1 — Pilot Launch

Grassroots Mobilization

Establish youth-led dialogue forums in our four founding counties: Nairobi, Bungoma, Kisumu and Nakuru.

Phase 2 — Expansion

Civic Academy Launch

Train youth peace champions in conflict prevention, constitutional literacy and public participation, scaling toward 20+ counties.

Phase 3 — Institutional Scale

Policy Collaboration

Partner with county peace committees to embed youth representatives directly into local decision-making forums.

How Change Happens

Four inputs. One outcome.

There are no shortcuts to trust. Every dialogue forum, every accountability session and every civic lesson feeds the same result — youth equipped to lead it.

One integrated system, not four programs. Each input reinforces the others and compounds into lasting trust over time.
