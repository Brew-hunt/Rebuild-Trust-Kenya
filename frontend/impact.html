Impact / 01

From four counties to a national model.

The pilot starts focused. The ambition is national: stronger youth participation, deeper trust and a repeatable preventive peacebuilding model that can travel county by county.

Where the work starts / 02

Nairobi. Bungoma. Kisumu. Nakuru.

The pilot counties form the first connected network for testing, learning and strengthening the model.

Three-year ambition / 03

Scale without losing the local work.

Growth is structured as a learning journey: pilot, refine, then expand the model with strong county coordination.

YEAR 01 / PILOT

Prove the model.

Train 200 young leaders and establish the pilot in four counties.

YEAR 02 / LEARN

Strengthen the network.

Use evidence, monitoring and county learning to improve the approach and deepen coordination.

YEAR 03 / SCALE

Take it wider.

Expand toward 20+ counties and engage 10,000+ young people at national scale.

What success looks like / 04

Not just numbers.

Success means stronger relationships, more responsible civic participation, better youth leadership and communities that can address tension before it becomes conflict.

Data helps us learn. Stories help us understand. Both are part of the impact picture.

Long-Term Outcome

“A generation of engaged, responsible citizens shaping Kenya's democracy peacefully.”
