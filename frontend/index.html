Our Approach

Peace, built before it breaks.

Rather than waiting for conflict or political tension to escalate, Rebuild Trust Kenya uses a preventive, continuous, youth-led, and integrated approach — combining dialogue, accountability, civic education, and peacebuilding.

01 / Trust

Trust

Sustained citizen–leader dialogue that rebuilds confidence between communities, elders, and decision-makers.

02 / Inclusion

Inclusion

Youth, elders, and civil society engaged together — so civic life reflects everyone it affects.

03 / Safety

Safety

Preventive peacebuilding that addresses tension early, before it becomes conflict.

What We Do

Dialogue, accountability, and civic education — working together.

Our activities are organized around citizen–leader dialogue, youth-led accountability, civic education and peacebuilding, and youth leadership development — each reinforcing the others across every county we work in.
