About Us / 01

A youth-led answer to a fragile trust.

Rebuild Trust Kenya exists to close the distance between citizens and the institutions meant to serve them — before that distance turns into conflict.

VISION / 02

Restoring unity and trust for a peaceful Kenya.

We imagine a Kenya where citizens and leaders trust one another enough to disagree without division — where dialogue, not tension, is the default way disputes are resolved.

MISSION / 03

Empowering young people to build trust, promote peace, and strengthen civic participation.

Young people are Kenya's largest constituency and its most consistent source of civic energy. We equip them with the skills, platforms, and standing to lead that work themselves — not as beneficiaries of peace, but as its architects.

Our Approach / 04

Preventive. Continuous. Youth-led. Integrated.

We combine dialogue, accountability, civic education and peacebuilding rather than waiting for conflict or political tension to escalate.

01

Preventive — Ahead of the fracture

We work before tension turns into conflict, building trust as ongoing maintenance, not emergency repair.

02

Continuous — Not a one-off event

Monthly dialogue forums and standing coordination structures keep the work going between crises, not just during them.

03

Youth-led — Led, not consulted

Young people design, facilitate, and monitor the work — with elders, leaders, and civil society as partners at the table.

04

Integrated — One system, not four silos

Dialogue surfaces issues, accountability follows through, civic education sustains understanding, and youth leadership carries the work county by county.

Long-Term Outcome

“A generation of engaged, responsible citizens shaping Kenya's democracy peacefully.”
