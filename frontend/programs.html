Programs / 01

Four activities.
One integrated model.

Each activity area feeds the others — dialogue surfaces what needs accountability, accountability needs civic education to sustain it, and all three depend on trained youth leadership to carry them county by county.

A

Activity 01

Citizen–Leader Dialogue

Regular, structured spaces where citizens and the people who represent them talk directly — across generations and across the usual divides.

  • Monthly citizen–leader dialogue forums.
  • Structured conversations around local priorities and concerns.
  • Cross-generational participation with elders, youth and leaders.
  • Follow-through that connects dialogue to accountability.
B

Activity 02

Youth-Led Accountability

Young people are equipped to ask better questions, follow public commitments and participate responsibly in civic processes.

  • Youth accountability forums and community conversations.
  • Practical civic participation skills.
  • Tracking and learning around commitments and outcomes.
  • Responsible engagement rather than adversarial politics.
C

Activity 03

Civic Education & Peacebuilding

Civic knowledge and peacebuilding reinforce each other: people are better able to participate constructively when they understand their rights, responsibilities and the warning signs of tension.

  • Civic education sessions for young people.
  • Conflict prevention and dialogue skills.
  • Peacebuilding awareness and community learning.
  • Early attention to local tensions before escalation.
D

Activity 04

Youth Leadership

We develop young leaders who can facilitate, mobilize, coordinate and learn from peacebuilding work in their own communities.

  • Training and development of 200 youth leaders in the pilot phase.
  • County-level coordination and facilitation.
  • Youth-led design, implementation and monitoring.
  • Leadership rooted in agency, collaboration and responsibility.

The common thread

Trust, Inclusion, and Safety.

These are the three areas every activity serves — making the model integrated rather than a collection of disconnected events.

01

Trust

Sustained citizen–leader dialogue rebuilds confidence between communities, elders and decision-makers.

02

Inclusion

Youth, elders and civil society engage together so civic life reflects everyone it affects.

03

Safety

Preventive peacebuilding addresses tension early, before it becomes conflict.

04

Learning

Monitoring, evaluation and learning help the model improve as it moves county by county.

Where it starts

Four pilot counties.

Nairobi · Bungoma · Kisumu · Nakuru

The pilot model is designed to expand toward 20+ counties by Year 3.
