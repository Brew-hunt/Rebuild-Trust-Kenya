Who can apply to be a Peace Champion?

Any youth aged 18–35 residing in Kenya with a passion for community building, civic literacy and peaceful conflict resolution.

Does RTK partner with local community groups?

Yes. We actively partner with community-based organizations (CBOs), faith-based groups and local youth councils.
